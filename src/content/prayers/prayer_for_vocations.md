---
title: Prayer for Vocations
order: 7
category: rosary
---

God our Father, please send us holy priests and religious, all
through the Eucharistic Heart of Jesus, all through the Immaculate
Heart of Mary, in union with St. Joseph.[^1]

Amen.

[^1]: A special prayer for vocations recited at [St. Henry Catholic Church](https://www.sthenry.org/), after the
    Fatima Prayer at the end of each decade.
